## Open points

1. **Freguesias total: 3259 vs 3258.** The CNE results list 3259 freguesia rows, but the Corvo row has no vote data. Corvo is a municipality with no freguesia in practice. The legal basis for Corvo (Estatuto Político-Administrativo dos Açores / Lei 169/99) was not opened. Choose one convention and state it in the app. We recommend 3259 with a note.
2. **Pre-2025 count 3092** was not found on an official page (DGAL portal autárquico, INE or DGT). Only Wikipedia states it. The CNE 2021 results give 3091 elected freguesias (no Corvo row).
3. **Number of freguesias restored / extinguished** by Lei 25-A/2025 (commonly "302 restored"; one source says 301) was not counted from the law annex. The annex PDF text extraction mixes columns. Net change +167 is derived only.
4. **Açores count** in CNE data is 156 including Corvo (155 without). Madeira 54. Not cross-checked against DGT CAOP2025 RAA/RAM datasets.
5. (Closed) The DGT CAOP2025 "distritos" collection gives 18 mainland districts. The 22 constituencies figure is derived from LEAR art. 12.º and this count.
6. **Censure motion of 8 Sept 2026:** the result is from news only. Vote counts conflict between sources. Check the parlamento.pt voting record before publishing any number.
7. **AR election date of the XVII Legislatura** (needed for the 6-month dissolution rule) was not verified.
8. CRP text used is the parlamento.pt version marked "VII Revisão Constitucional [2005]". No later revision is shown on that page. A revision process may be in progress in the AR; this was not checked.
9. LEOAL copy on cne.pt is the 2021-06-04 version. Later amendments (if any) were not checked. The 4-year mandate is also confirmed in Lei 169/99 art. 75.º.
10. presidencia.pt biography page URL returned "Página não encontrada" when fetched directly; the posse news page was used instead.
