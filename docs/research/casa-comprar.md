# Research: "comprar casa" cost calculator — verified legal values (mainland Portugal)

verifiedOn for every row: **2026-09-12**. Local copies of the sources are in `scratchpad/comprar/`.

Verification levels: **primary** (text read in the law or an official publication / official consolidated code on Portal das Finanças), **derived** (computed from a primary rule), **secondary** (2 reputable secondary sources agree), **uncertain**.

## 0. Corrections to the task premises

1. The IMT Jovem rule is **CIMT art. 9.º n.os 2–5**, plus the **art. 17.º n.º 1 b)** table and **art. 11.º n.º 8** (loss of the benefit). It is not art. 8.º (art. 8.º is about purchases for resale).
2. The garantia pública price cap is a **fixed 450 000 €** (DL 44/2024 art. 2.º e)). It is not the 4.º escalão of IMT. The 4.º escalão (330 539 € in 2026) is the ceiling for the **registo emolumentos exemption** (RERN art. 28.º n.º 37). The IMT Jovem 0 % ceiling is the **1.º escalão of table b)**, which is also 330 539 €. These are three ceilings in three different laws. Only two of them have the same number in 2026.
3. The Imposto do Selo benefit for young buyers is a **dedução à coleta** (a deduction from the tax due), not an isenção (CIS art. 7.º-A). The deduction is capped at 0,8 % × 330 539 = 2 644,31 €.
4. Imposto do Selo on the housing loan: there is **no young-buyer exemption**. The 4 % on interest (verba 17.3.1) is **not charged** on housing loans (CIS art. 7.º n.º 1 l)).
5. BdP rules changed on **1 Aug 2026** (Recomendação Macroprudencial n.º 1/2026). DSTI is now **45 %**. The 100 % LTV for bank-owned property is **eliminated**. Maturity is **40 years (≤35 y) / 35 years (>35 y)**. The average-maturity rule is **eliminated**.
6. New since May 2026: **DL 97/2026** added CIMT art. 17.º n.º 10 (**7,5 % flat IMT for non-resident buyers** of housing). It also changed the IMT payment deadline to 30 days (art. 36.º).

## 1. IMT — CIMT art. 17.º

Legal basis for the 2026 limits: **Lei n.º 73-A/2025, de 30 de dezembro (OE2026), art. 83.º**. It changed only the bracket limits (about +2 %). The rates and average rates come from Lei 82/2023 and DL 48-A/2024. The parcelas a abater are the AT's official values from **Ofício Circulado n.º 40129/2026, de 06/01**.

Tax formula (brackets 1–5): `IMT = V × taxa marginal − parcela a abater`. For the "taxa única" brackets: `IMT = V × taxa`.
V = the higher of the contract price and the VPT (CIMT art. 12.º n.º 1).

## Open points

1. **Garantia pública after 31-12-2026**: I found no extension. Recheck in Q4 2026 (Portaria 236-A/2024/1 art. 5.º n.º 5 allows an extension).
2. **DL 97/2026 art. 17.º n.º 10 start date**: derived as 25-05-2026 from the Lei 74/98 default. There is no explicit article. The calculator needs a "non-resident" flag. The exceptions b)/c) are refunds after payment, not an up-front rate.
3. **RERN amounts (250 € / 500 €)**: I read them in the PGDL consolidated text, not in the DR consolidated text (JS page). I did not open Lei 53/2026 (19/08). Its article lists suggest it did not change arts. 21.º, 27.º-A or 28.º.
4. **Escritura / DPA / bank commissions**: private fees. There is no primary value. I found only one secondary source (Doutor Finanças). Keep them as user inputs or a labelled estimate.
5. **IMI municipal rate**: 0,3–0,45 % is the legal range. The real rate per municipality comes from the AT "Taxas IMI" list. I did not collect it.
