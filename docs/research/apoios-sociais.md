## Open points

1. **Guide vs Portaria discrepancy (abono, 4.º escalão, 2 titulares):** G-AF p. 5 shows "9,28 €"; Portaria 60/2026/1 art. 3.º a) sets **39,28 €**. Use the Portaria value. The guide value is likely a typo.
2. **Majoração "2nd child" semantics:** The Portaria ties the majoração to children ≤ 36 months in agregados with 2 or more than 2 titulares (DL 176/2003 art. 14.º-A). The guide text says "2 or more children ≤ 36 months". Check art. 14.º-A n.º 1 in the consolidated DL 176/2003 before coding the condition.
3. **1.º/2.º escalão age bands:** The Portaria has only two bands (≤ 36 / > 36 months) for escalões 1 and 2; the guide table shows three rows with equal values. Escalões 3 and 4 have three bands.
4. **Garantia para a Infância 2026 monthly value:** Only the annual 1 528,00 € is primary. The 127,33 €/month figure is derived (÷ 12). The 2026 guide (4004) at seg-social.pt returned an empty page / 404; only the 2025 version (v1.6) was read.
5. **Garantia threshold for 2026 new requests:** The 2026 abono guide gives only 2 495,37 € (2024 income, IAS 2024). The 2 560,25 € figure (2025 income, IAS 2025) is derived.
6. **10 % reduction after 180 days (desemprego):** Absent from the 2026 ISS guide. Revocation (Lei 114/2017) is confirmed only by non-official sources. The consolidated DL 220/2006 on diariodarepublica.pt is a JS app and could not be read.
7. **1,15 × IAS minimum (617,70 €):** Stated in G-SD, but the guide does not name the legal provision. Find the amending diploma to DL 220/2006 art. 29.º.
8. **Article numbers for DL 220/2006 (arts. 22.º, 37.º):** Taken from the brief; the rule contents match G-SD, but the consolidated article text was not opened. Arts. 28.º and 29.º were confirmed in AR-28 (2014 redaction).
9. **10 % majoração (desemprego) legal basis:** AR-28 shows it in OE 2014 art. 116.º (temporary). The current permanent basis was not verified.
10. **CSI 2025 value:** ≈ 7 568 € is derived from the 6,24 % rate; the exact value in Portaria 311/2024/1 was not opened. The brief's 7 188 € is not the 2025 value. The 2027 retirement age is set by Portaria 476/2025/1, but its value was not read.
11. **Typos in ISS guides:** G-SSD p. 10 shows "537,50 €" once (should be 537,13 €); G-SD p. 4 shows "20256". Do not copy these.
12. **RMMG 920,00 €:** Confirmed through ISS guides citing DL 139/2025; the DL text itself was not opened.
