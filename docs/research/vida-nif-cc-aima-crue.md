## 1. NIF — Número de Identificação Fiscal (residents and non-residents)

**Who needs it.** Any person, Portuguese or foreign, resident or non-resident, who is subject to tax obligations or wants to exercise rights before the AT (Autoridade Tributária e Aduaneira). It is needed for employment, contracts, bank accounts, social security. One person can only hold one NIF. Portuguese citizens with a Cartão de Cidadão already have the NIF attributed with the card. The NIF has 9 digits and does not change when the person moves from "não residente" to "residente".

**Where.**
- **In person (the applicant):** any Serviço de Finanças or Loja do Cidadão, **appointment required** ("Atendimento por Marcação" on Portal das Finanças, option "Para pedido de NIF ou alteração de morada para pessoas sem cartão de cidadão", or CAT +351 217 206 707, business days 9h–19h).
- **Online (e-balcão):** only through a **representante legal / procurador** (a figure distinct from the representante fiscal), path "Registo Contribuinte > Identific > Atrib/Alter NIF-Singulares", with scanned documents and power of attorney. The NIF is then attributed as **não residente** (address abroad).
- gov.pt adds that foreigners with an address in Portugal can request NIF + NISS + número de utente SNS in a single request (not opened in detail — see Open points).

**Documents.**
- *Non-resident (any nationality):* civil ID or valid passport; document with foreign address (unless it is on the ID). Third-country nationals applying in person must show the entry visa in the passport (except if a Schengen/EU visa is in the passport or the nationality is visa-exempt). If a mandatário applies: original or certified copy of the ID, address proof, and procuração (signature recognition not required if given to advogado/solicitador). Foreign-language documents need a certified translation.
- *Resident — EU/EEE/Andorra/Suíça:* ID/passport + **Certificado de Registo de Cidadão da União Europeia** (CRUE) from the câmara municipal.
- *Resident — third-country national:* ID/passport + one of: AIMA declaration of international protection request / temporary protection title; valid **título de autorização de residência**; AIMA appointment proof or receipt of a pending concession/renewal. If those documents lack the address: deed of purchase, lease contract, employment contract or document from a public entity.

**Cost 2026.** **Free** ("O pedido do NIF é gratuito" — gov.pt guide updated 02/09/2026).

**Representante fiscal vs. electronic notifications (AT rule, primary).**
- At the moment of attributing a NIF as **não residente**, appointing a representante fiscal is **not mandatory**.
- If the non-resident then establishes a **relação jurídica tributária** (e.g. owns a vehicle registered in PT, owns real estate in PT, signs an employment contract for work in PT, or carries out self-employed activity in PT), they must, **within 15 days** (from that relationship, or from communicating an address in a third country):
  - appoint a representante fiscal in Portugal, **or**
  - adhere to the *sistema de notificações e citações eletrónicas* of Portal das Finanças, **or** to the *caixa postal eletrónica* **ViaCTT**.
- For residents of an **EU country, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein** and Andorran citizens resident in Andorra, both options are **optional**.
- **Exception:** a third-country resident who carries out **self-employed activity** in Portugal must appoint a **representante fiscal de IVA** (a VAT-registered person resident in PT) **before starting the activity**; adhering to e-notifications does **not** replace it.
- Who can be representative: individuals or companies with domicile in Portugal; a foreign citizen can be representative only with a valid residence permit and registered as resident with the AT.
- Penalty for missing a mandatory appointment: coima **€75 to €7.500** (art. 124.º RGIT) and loss of the right to complain/appeal before the AT.
- Change to residente: once >183 days in any 12-month period, or habitual dwelling in PT, communicate residence within **60 days** (e-balcão "Alteração Morada/Singulares" or in person).

**Deadline / processing.** No deadline to request. Processing time not published (uncertain).

## 2. Cartão de Cidadão (CC)

**Who.** Mandatory for every Portuguese citizen (in PT or abroad) from **20 days of age** or when they need a public service. Brazilian citizens with *Estatuto de Igualdade de Direitos e Deveres* (Tratado de Porto Seguro) and a valid residence card may also request it; their CC has the same validity as their residence card.

**Where.**
- First CC of a baby up to 1 year: **free**, online via *Registo de nascimento online* or at the maternity hospital. From 1 year, first CC at an IRN balcão.
- Replacement of old Bilhete de Identidade: IRN balcão (bring BI, cartão de contribuinte, Segurança Social, utente de saúde, eleitor cards).
- Renewal in person: balcões das conservatórias do registo civil and Lojas do Cidadão (**no prior appointment needed**), Espaços Cidadão (from age 25), Lojas RIAC (Açores), consulates abroad.
- **From age 25, without leaving home:** *renovação automática com entrega em casa* (no data changes) or *renovação online* (to change name, address or contacts).
- Address change: online **free**; at IRN balcão **€3**; it updates the electoral register.

**When to renew.** If the card expires **in the next six months**, is expired, lost/stolen, printed data (photo, signature) must change, identity elements changed in the birth record, or the chip is damaged.

**Validity (justica.gov.pt, updated 27 Aug 2026).**
- Under 25: **5 years**.
- 25 and over: **10 years**.
- Exception: cards issued **13 Aug 2021 – 10 Jun 2024** are valid until **3 August 2031** (EU Regulation 2019/1157; new card model issued since 11 June 2024).
- **No "permanent/lifetime validity from 60" rule appears** on the IRN/justica.gov.pt page. The premise is **not confirmed** (see Open points).
- Provisional CC: valid **90 days**, only in Portugal.

**Cost 2026 — Portugal continental.**

- **Açores:** same prices; urgent = 4 dias úteis.
- **Madeira:** same prices; urgent = 5 dias úteis.
- **Abroad (consulates):** online renewal >5y €20,70; online renewal ≤5y (loss/theft) €18; normal <25 €20; normal 25+ €23; urgent <25 €45; urgent 25+ €48.
- The "online discount" is implicit: €16,20 online/automatic vs. €18 in person for 25+ (derived: 10% less).
- Fee waiver possible for proven economic insufficiency (Segurança Social / Junta de Freguesia document, or public social-assistance institution declaration).
- Payment at balcão: card, cash or certified cheque (national account).

**Processing.** Urgent 3/4/5 working days (continente/Açores/Madeira); extremely urgent same/next day. Normal delivery time not stated on the page (uncertain).

## 3. AIMA — residence permits for non-EU (third-country) nationals

**General structure.** Most routes are two-step: (1) **residence visa** requested at a Portuguese consulate (https://vistos.mne.gov.pt), which includes an automatic AIMA appointment; (2) **autorização de residência (AR)** requested at a **Loja AIMA** by appointment (online platform "em fase de implementação"). Temporary AR for professional activity is valid **2 years** from issue and renewable for **successive 3-year periods**. Common documents: valid passport, valid visa, sworn declaration of address plus property certificate or landlord declaration, proof of NIF (AT) and Segurança Social registration; no criminal conviction > 1 year in prison; no entry ban. Renewals go through the AIMA **Portal de Renovações**.

### 3.2 Manifestação de interesse — status 2026
- **DL 37-A/2024 (3 June 2024)** revoked arts. 88.º n.º 2 and 6, 89.º n.º 2, 4 and 5 and 81.º n.º 6–7; in force the day after publication; **does not apply to procedures started up to its entry into force**.
- **Lei n.º 40/2024 (7 Nov 2024)** changed that transitional regime (search snippet: covers people already registered in Social Security with contributions before the change, to complete 12 months) — **content not opened** (secondary).
- **Lei n.º 61/2025 (22 Oct 2025), art. 4.º** added art. 3.º n.º 3 to DL 37-A/2024: requests under "alínea b) do número anterior" had to be submitted **"impreterivelmente, até ao dia 31 de dezembro de 2025, sob pena de caducidade"** (primary).
- Result for the app (derived): **no new manifestação de interesse can be filed in 2026**; the transitional window closed on 31 Dec 2025. Pending procedures started before 4 June 2024 continue.
- AT folheto confirms: AR procedures based on manifestações de interesse were revoked; procedures started until 4 June 2024 are safeguarded.

## 4. EU/EEE/Swiss citizens — CRUE and permanent residence

**Who.** Nationals of EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Andorra and Switzerland who stay **more than 3 months** (and up to 5 years). Up to 3 months only a valid ID/passport is needed. Conditions (art. 7.º Lei 37/2006): employed/self-employed in PT, **or** sufficient resources + health insurance (when the home state requires it of Portuguese citizens), **or** enrolled student with resources + insurance; or family member accompanying/joining such a citizen.

**When.** **Within 30 days after 3 months** from entry into Portugal.

**Where.** **Câmara Municipal of the area of residence** (Lei 37/2006, art. 14.º). Porto: in person with appointment (Linha Porto 220 100 220) or online form. Cascais: in person at Loja Cascais; physical presence required, no representation.

**Documents.** Valid ID card or passport; sworn declaration (compromisso de honra) of meeting one of the art. 7.º conditions; proof of residence in the municipality (Porto accepts: atestado da Junta de Freguesia, lease + AT e-receipt or Modelo II, purchase deed/land registry certificate, certidão de domicílio fiscal, student residence declaration). The câmara may ask for supporting originals (work contract, enrolment). Family members: proof of relationship, the CRUE of the EU citizen, proof of dependency when relevant.

**Cost 2026 (national — Portaria n.º 1334-D/2010, art. 3.º, as amended by Portaria n.º 13/2024; effects from 29 Oct 2023).** Applies to CRUE **and** the permanent residence certificate.

- The AIMA table PDF also lists "Emissão de documento a menores de 6 anos €7,50" and "Serviço Externo €40" — these lines are **not** in the Portaria 13/2024 text read (source: AIMA table only).
- CRUE fee revenue is split between municipalities and AIMA (Portaria art. 4.º).
- So the common "15 €" figure is correct only for digital 25+ or in-person under 25; **in person 25+ pays €18**.

**Validity / processing.** Cascais: CRUE valid **5 years**; afterwards renewal via AIMA. Porto: in person "serviço na hora"; online up to 10 working days. AIMA page says "Prazo mínimo de emissão, 1 mês" (divergent).

**Permanent residence (after 5 years).**
- *Certificado de Residência Permanente* for EU citizens and family: after **5 consecutive years** of legal residence; request **at AIMA** via the **Portal de Renovações** (the channel since **1 July 2026**, replacing the Formulário de Contacto; requests via the form until 30 June 2026 are kept); biometrics appointment notified by AIMA if needed.
- Documents: valid BI/CC/passport; the CRUE; proof of address change if applicable; request form.
- When: before the registration certificate expires. Deadline for decision: **3 months (maximum)**.
- Fee: same Portaria 13/2024 table (€15 digital / €18 in person for 25+).
